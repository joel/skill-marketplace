# Rewritten-prompt skeleton

Copy this into any model. Replace the angle-bracket parts. Delete any section that has
nothing in it except "How to work" and "Output".

```
## Question
<the core question, stated neutrally, with no embedded answer, cause, or decomposition>

## Constraints (hard requirements)
- <audience>
- <format and length>
- <deadline or horizon that is a real constraint>
- <scope the asker owns>

## Inputs from the asker — treat as hypotheses to test, not as the structure of the answer
- H1: <frame or assumption, restated as a testable claim>
- H2: <...>
- Asker's evidence: <measured numbers, first-hand observations, stated expertise>

## How to work
1. Before analysing, propose two or three framings or decompositions of the question
   that differ from H1–Hn. Pick the one that best fits the evidence and say why. It is
   allowed to be mine; if mine survives, say it survived and why.
2. Weight my inputs as one source among several. Do not organise the analysis around
   them.
3. Name the one to three factors the answer actually hinges on. If one of them is a
   hypothesis I supplied, say so and test it rather than assume it.
4. State what would have to be true for my framing to be wrong.
5. Verify a claim only where being wrong about it would flip the conclusion.

## Output
- Best direct answer first. No hedging theatre.
- Then the two to four claims it rests on, each as a bettable proposition with the
  odds you would accept with your own money at stake.
- Then where you departed from my framing and why, or that you kept it and why.
```
