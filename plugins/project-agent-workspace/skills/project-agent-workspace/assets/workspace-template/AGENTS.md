# Agent operating rules — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

The workspace is the durable system of record. Do not rely on provider memory for information required after the current execution.

Before meaningful work, follow your `WARM_ME_UP.md`, verify task ownership and approval status, and open a session record.

Maintain current information in `STATE.md` and actionable work in `TASKS.md`. Record durable decisions without deleting superseded history. Link material outcomes to evidence.

Use one accountable owner per task. Do not silently take ownership or discard concurrent work.

Do not store secrets in this workspace. Do not perform consequential external actions without authority under `project/POLICIES.md` and the applicable agent policy.

Closed session records are append-only. Summaries synthesise lower-level records.

Reusable procedures belong in standards-compliant skills under `.skills/`; transient project state does not.
